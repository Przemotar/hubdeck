Everything the deck can do

Every module, live

Keys, the round dial screen, knobs, faders, rollers and the edge light, found and placed as they sit on your base, while it runs.

Profiles that follow you

Your home profile, and an app's own profile takes over while it is in front. Pages, holds, double presses, modes.

Stream Deck plugins

A Plugins tab with about 300 plugins made for Stream Deck that install with one click (the open ones on Elgato Marketplace, the OpenAction catalog and GitHub), the plugins your Stream Deck app already has, and any plugin file you open with HubDeck. Of 53 popular ones checked, 44 start and draw on Windows; tell us when one does not work.

Your apps on the deck

Your own tools can show their state (an agent waiting, a build running) and offer actions through a small HTTP contract.

Lights that mean something

One style for the whole deck, with reactions: a call, a muted mic, an agent that needs you.

Light and private

About one percent of a core in the tray. No account, no cloud, no telemetry.

A deck made for you, in minutes

Say what you do most at the computer, and HubDeck makes a deck for your modules and your apps at once, then walks you through it on the real hardware: the control to try lights up, and you keep the parts you like. No AI needed. Want more? The assistant, running on your own Claude Code, builds on it, or go control by control through a gallery of ideas.

Written from scratch, in the open

The USB protocol of every module was worked out and checked on real hardware, then the app was built around it with Claude Code. The code, the protocol and the story are public: take it, change it, extend it, with or without AI.

Questions

Is HubDeck free?

Yes. It is free and open source (MIT or Apache-2.0), with no account, no cloud and no telemetry. It is made by a MasterHUB owner and is not affiliated with Cooler Master.

Is it an alternative to the official MasterHUB software?

Yes. HubDeck drives the whole Cooler Master MasterHUB deck by itself: keys, dial, knobs, faders, rollers and the edge light, with profiles that follow the app in front, Stream Deck plugins and a tour that sets up your deck in minutes.

Which MasterHUB modules does it support?

All of them, on Windows 10 and 11 and on macOS 12 or later: the base with its edge light, the 15 LCD keys, the dial with its round screen, the 3 knobs, the 5 faders and the 2 rollers. Attach and take off modules while it runs; it finds where each one sits. One base at a time for now.

Do I need to uninstall the official app? Can I go back?

No uninstall is needed: quit the official app first, since two apps cannot drive the deck at once. To go back, quit HubDeck from the tray and start the official app; your modules are unchanged. Profiles exported from the official app (.mhz) can be imported (beta).

Can it damage or brick my modules?

No. HubDeck never writes firmware. Commands that could rewrite serial numbers, reset a module or flash firmware are not part of its code, and reports carrying them are refused.

Can I use Stream Deck plugins on the MasterHUB?

Yes. The Plugins tab installs about 300 plugins made for Stream Deck with one click, and HubDeck can use the plugins your Stream Deck app already has. HubDeck drives the MasterHUB only, not Stream Deck hardware.

Does it work on a Mac?

Yes, on macOS 12 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Download the .dmg and drag HubDeck to Applications.

Is AI required?

No. The tour, the editor and everything else work without it. The optional deck assistant runs on your own Claude Code.

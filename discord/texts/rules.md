**Rules**

1. Be kind. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
2. English in the channels here, so everyone can follow. Posts in other languages are fine in a thread when the people in it share the language.
3. Bugs go to GitHub issues, where they are tracked and fixed: <https://github.com/Przemotar/hubdeck/issues/new?template=bug.yml>. Attach the diagnostics file (Help and feedback > Save diagnostics).
4. No ads, no selling, no invites to other servers.
5. Download HubDeck only from its GitHub releases: <https://github.com/Przemotar/hubdeck/releases>. A file offered anywhere else is not ours.
6. No NSFW content, no slurs, no spam. Discord's AutoMod removes some of it; moderators remove the rest.

HubDeck is a community project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cooler Master.

The HubDeck bot reads the messages here so the maker gets a summary of what people ask and propose. The summaries are made with the help of AI. The bot does not chat and does not answer on its own; what it posts was written or approved by the maker.

# HubDeck 0.2.0: the living deck
The keys of the deck no longer have to be fifteen separate squares. 0.2.0 draws one moving picture through all of them, behind your icons, and the picture answers your presses. Twelve presets to start with: click **Living deck** under the deck in the window, try one on your deck, keep it with **Apply**. Your key styles stay as they are.

For Halloween there are two of them: Hollow Hill (a manor on a knoll, a harvest moon, a graveyard in the fog) and Lantern Hollow (jack-o'-lanterns, an owl, a black cat, a witch across the moon now and then). The others: a golden autumn forest, a mushroom hunt, Fuji with cherry blossom, a koi pond, a Tokyo street in the rain, code rain, synthwave, a cyber rig, a nebula and a music spectrum that follows what your speakers play. The edge, knobs and faders take the preset's colours.

Also new: any preset can be your screensaver, a look editor for keys (templates, patterns, badges, styles), a key's look that follows what happens (CPU, music, a muted mic), icon packs in and out, sharper keys, and pictures on the keys about twice as fast.

This is also the first post on HubDeck's Discord. Rate the presets with ⭐ in #presets, post and vote on ideas in #ideas, ask in #help. Bugs still go to GitHub issues, with the diagnostics file.

# Code of conduct

HubDeck follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and level of
experience, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not
accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere the project is represented.

Report a problem privately to the maintainer, Przemysław Tarkowski, at
przemotar+hubdeck@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially; the maintainer may remove
comments, lock threads or block people who break these rules.
